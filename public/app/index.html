what clipping ai does

Clipping AI is a clip engine that turns long recordings (podcasts, webinars, livestreams, interviews) into vertical 9:16 clips for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

faster-whisper transcribes with word-level timestamps, PySceneDetect maps the scene cuts, and Google Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite scores the transcript to pull out the 3 to 15 strongest moments of 15 to 60 seconds each. Each one is cut with FFmpeg and reframed to 9:16 using MediaPipe face tracking.

FAQ: free clipping, watermarks and limits

Is Clipping AI actually free?
Yes. There is a free plan: your first video is clipped free up to 60 minutes, then you get 20 minutes a month with a watermark and no card. Paid plans from $12/month remove the watermark and raise the volume to 100, 300 or 750 minutes. An NVIDIA GPU clips an 8-minute video in about 50 seconds, the AI keys are included, auto-posting to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube is already wired up, and your clips stay retrievable from any browser.
Do the clips have a watermark?
Only on the free Cloud tier. Clips made on the free plan of Clipping AI Cloud carry a small watermark; every paid plan, from $12/month, has none, and upgrading wipes the mark from clips you already made.
Is a card needed to start?
No. The free tier of Clipping AI Cloud needs no card: your first video is clipped free up to 60 minutes, then you get 20 free minutes every month, watermarked. You only add a card if you pick a paid plan, from $12/month.
How many clips do I get from one video?
Between 3 and 15, each 15 to 60 seconds long. The engine scores every moment and keeps the ones that stand on their own, so the count depends on your source, not a fixed quota.
Can I clip someone else's YouTube video?
Only if you own it or have the rights or permission to use it. Before processing, Clipping AI asks you to confirm you own the content or hold the rights, and you are responsible for what you submit. Fair use depends on the country and on how the clip is used, so it is never guaranteed; reuploading someone else's video without permission is copyright infringement, and platforms remove it and strike the account. Clip your own videos, channels you manage, or footage you have licensed.
What is Clipping AI and how does it work?

How does Clipping AI compare to Opus Clip?
Both do Highlight finder and smart vertical cropping. Clipping AI adds voice dubbing into 30+ languages (with your own ElevenLabs key) plus an AI ad generator with lip-synced actors. Opus Clip is closed source and cloud only, starting at $15/month as of October 2026, and it ships a larger caption library. Clipping AI starts at $12/month. Full comparison at /alternatives/opus-clip.
How does the smart vertical cropping work?
TRACK mode follows a single subject with MediaPipe face detection and a YOLOv8 fallback, damped so the crop holds still inside a safe zone rather than chasing every head movement. GENERAL mode handles group shots and landscapes by keeping the full width over a blurred backdrop. A speaker tracker prevents the crop from flipping between people and holds position through brief occlusions.
Can Clipping AI translate and dub videos?
Yes, with your own ElevenLabs API key, into more than 30 languages, preserving the original speaker's voice characteristics. After dubbing, the audio is re-transcribed so the burned-in subtitles are in the target language rather than the original.

You describe the business or paste a website URL, and the AI writes a script, generates a lip-synced actor with voiceover, adds b-roll and subtitles, with the hook spoken in the first seconds. Low Cost mode is about $0.65 per video and Premium is about $2.00. It works for restaurants, e-commerce, coaching, local businesses and apps, not only software.

Yes. Clipping AI has a native MCP server at mcp.clippingai.local/mcp plus a REST API with per-user keys and completion webhooks, so an agent can submit a video URL, wait for processing, list the clips and publish them. The endpoint is always on, with the API key created in your account page in one click. Full guide at /mcp.
